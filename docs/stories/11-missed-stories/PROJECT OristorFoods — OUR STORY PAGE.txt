PROJECT CINNAMON — OUR STORY PAGE
Interactive Storytelling Experience
1. Design Direction

Create a premium, immersive Our Story / About Us page for The Oristor.

Use the linked Kumo Interactive Matcha landing page as UX inspiration only:

Kumo Interactive Matcha — Dribbble reference

DO NOT copy Kumo's:

Layout
Graphics
Illustrations
Typography
Colour combinations
Product presentation
Animations
Text
Composition
Branding

Instead, understand the underlying concept:

Turn a normal About Us page into an interactive visual story.

The user should feel that they are travelling through the story of Sri Lankan food and The Oristor, rather than scrolling through blocks of company information.

2. Core Concept
“FROM OUR ROOTS TO YOUR TABLE”

The page should tell the Oristor story through a sequence of visual chapters.

The experience should communicate:

Sri Lanka → Heritage → Ingredients → Traditional Recipes → Craftsmanship → Quality → Innovation → The World

The user should discover each stage naturally while scrolling.

3. Hero — “A Taste of Where We Come From”

Create an immersive hero section.

Suggested headline:

OUR STORY
A Taste of Sri Lanka,
Crafted for the World.

Supporting text:

Rooted in Sri Lanka's rich culinary traditions, The Oristor brings authentic flavours, time-honoured recipes and carefully selected ingredients to kitchens around the world.

Use a large cinematic Sri Lankan food image/video as the visual hero.

Possible imagery:

Sri Lankan landscape
spices
chilli
cinnamon
traditional food preparation
fresh ingredients
Oristor products

The hero should feel premium, emotional and cinematic, not like a corporate About Us page.

4. Interactive Story — “Where It Begins”

As the user scrolls, introduce Sri Lanka visually.

CHAPTER 01

THE LAND

Show:

Sri Lankan landscape
spice-growing regions
tropical ingredients
chilli

vegetables

Use subtle parallax movement.

Text:

Born from Sri Lanka

Our story begins in Sri Lanka — an island where food is deeply connected to culture, family and tradition.

The background imagery should slowly move while the text remains anchored.

5. CHAPTER 02 — “The Flavours We Grew Up With”

Transition from landscape into ingredients.

Create floating / layered ingredient imagery.

Examples:

Chilli
Garlic

Each ingredient can briefly reveal a small story.

Example:

Sweet, sour and unmistakably Sri Lankan.

SPICES
The foundation of our flavour.

Do not overuse text.

6. CHAPTER 03 — “Tradition in Every Recipe”

Now transition from ingredients to finished food.

Show authentic Sri Lankan dishes and traditional preparation.

Headline:

Recipes With a Memory

Supporting text:

Some flavours are more than recipes. They are memories passed from one generation to another.

Use large food photography with subtle motion.

Possible visual transition:

Ingredient → preparation → finished dish → Oristor product

This should be one of the emotional highlights of the page.

7. CHAPTER 04 — “From Tradition to The Oristor”

Now introduce the company.

Use the actual Oristor product photography.

Headline:

From Our Kitchen to Yours

Text based on the approved company profile:

Oristor Food Products (Pvt) Ltd is a Sri Lankan manufacturer and trader of ready-to-eat food products and spices.

Our heritage is rooted in Sri Lanka's culinary traditions, carried to kitchens worldwide through time-honoured recipes.

Do not invent company history, dates or achievements that are not documented.

Use real product images wherever possible.

8. Interactive Product Story

Instead of a conventional product grid, create a horizontal or pinned scrolling sequence.

As the user scrolls:

Pickles → Sambols → Thokku → Chilli Pastes → Seafood → Masalas → Spices

The central product changes while supporting ingredients move around it.

This is where we take the core interaction idea from Kumo.

For example:

MANGO PICKLE

Mango imagery appears.

Then:

Mango → spices → preparation → finished product

Then transition to:

GARLIC CURRY

Garlic → chilli → spices → finished product

Then:

RED ONION THOKKU

etc.

The interaction should feel like discovering flavour, not browsing a product catalogue.

13. Animation Philosophy

Animation must tell the story, not simply decorate the page.

Use Framer Motion according to the existing Cinnamon architecture.

Preferred effects:

Scroll-triggered reveals
Parallax photography
Image scaling
Ingredient floating
Product transitions
Horizontal product storytelling
Mask reveals
Subtle text movement
Layered depth
Sticky/pinned storytelling sections

Avoid:

excessive bouncing
flashy transitions
unnecessary 3D
excessive particle effects
slow animations that hurt usability
animation on every element
Rule:

Every animation must have a storytelling or UX purpose.

14. Cinnamon Visual Language

Strictly maintain the existing Project Cinnamon design system.

15. Important — Brand Authenticity

Use actual Oristor product images and approved company information wherever available.

Do not generate imaginary products.

Do not alter:

Product labels
Logo
Packaging
Product colours
Product texture
Product names

Product photography can be creatively composed, cropped, masked or animated, but the actual product identity must remain intact.

16. Responsive Experience

The experience must work beautifully on:

Desktop → Tablet → Mobile

Do not simply shrink the desktop animation for mobile.

For mobile:

simplify complex interactions
reduce simultaneous animations
convert horizontal sequences into vertical storytelling where necessary
maintain readable typography
maintain fast loading
respect prefers-reduced-motion

Target WCAG AA accessibility in accordance with the Cinnamon specification.

Create reusable components such as:

18. Most Important Instruction to Claude
DO NOT BUILD A CLONE OF KUMO.

Use Kumo only to understand the design philosophy:

Product + imagery + motion + scrolling = storytelling

Then reinterpret that philosophy through:

Sri Lankan heritage + Oristor products + ingredients + craftsmanship + quality + global ambition

The finished page should feel unmistakably ORISTOR / PROJECT CINNAMON, not like a matcha website with the colours changed.

My recommendation

I think this direction is better than a conventional About Us page for Project Cinnamon.

Your existing profile already gives us the factual foundation: Oristor's mission is to craft authentic Sri Lankan condiments and spices and bring them to kitchens worldwide, while the vision is to become a global front-runner in the category.

So rather than making the page:

About Us → Mission → Vision → Values → Certifications → Contact

we make it:

SRI LANKA → INGREDIENTS → HERITAGE → RECIPES → ORISTOR → QUALITY → VALUES → WORLD

That gives Claude a story to build, rather than just another page to fill with text.

And importantly, it fits the existing Cinnamon vision of creating a world-class digital destination for authentic Sri Lankan food, rather than simply an e-commerce website.
