STORY-069 — Premium Contact & Enquiry Experience

Title: ORISTOR Contact Us — Premium Contact & Business Enquiry Page
Priority: High
Type: UX / Frontend / Lead Generation
Target: /contact-us

1. Objective

Replace the current basic Contact Us experience with a premium, modern, highly visual ORISTOR contact page.

The page should communicate:

ORISTOR is a premium Sri Lankan food brand, manufacturer and international food partner.

The page must make it easy for visitors to:

Contact ORISTOR
Ask a product question
Make a general enquiry
Request wholesale/distributor information
Make an export enquiry
Discuss business opportunities
Contact customer support

The design should be inspired by premium Awwwards contact-page patterns, but must be an original ORISTOR design and must not copy another website's layout, artwork or text. The reference style emphasizes large typography, conversational forms, whitespace and strong CTA hierarchy.

2. Design Direction

Create a premium editorial contact experience, consistent with the existing Cinnamon/ORISTOR design system.

Visual personality
Premium
Authentic
Sri Lankan
Warm
Modern
Minimal
Confident
International/export-ready

Do not create a generic corporate contact page.

Avoid:

Generic Bootstrap-style form
Large boxed cards everywhere
Excessive borders
Excessive gradients
Overuse of icons
Crowded layout
Stock-looking corporate imagery
3. Hero Section

Create a large editorial hero.

Suggested heading

Let's talk about good food.

Alternative:

Let's connect.

Supporting copy:

Whether you're looking for authentic Sri Lankan flavours, interested in becoming a retail partner, or simply want to talk to us, we'd love to hear from you.

Use very large typography with strong visual impact.

The hero should occupy approximately 60–75vh on desktop.

Include subtle ORISTOR food imagery / product photography without overwhelming the typography.

Use the existing ORISTOR brand colours and typography.

Never trust client-side validation alone.
