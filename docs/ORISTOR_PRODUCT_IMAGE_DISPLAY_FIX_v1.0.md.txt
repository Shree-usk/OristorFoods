Issue: Product images are not visible in the product selection area

7.	Ensure images load correctly on desktop and mobile, with appropriate sizing and aspect ratios.
8.	Test the product selection area with multiple products and variants, including products with missing images.
Important: Preserve existing product images, labels, product data, and the current website design. Implement the fix on a feature branch, run the relevant tests, and open a pull request. Do not deploy to production without approval.
